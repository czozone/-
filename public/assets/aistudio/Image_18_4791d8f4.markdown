## Brand & Style
The design system combines the clinical precision of professional salon operations with the reassuring warmth of premium pet hospitality. It bridges two distinct user mindsets: pet parents seeking safety, tenderness, and transparency for their companions; and professional groomers requiring high-density, ergonomic scheduling, intake tracking, and signature verification.

The visual style blends **Modern Organic Tactility** with **Clean Ergonomic Utility**:
- **Warmth over Clinical Coldness:** Rich cream backdrops and terracotta warmth replace sterile, icy medical tones, evoking a boutique, spa-like atmosphere.
- **Friendly Authority:** Rounded geometry, generous tap targets, and soft ambient shading remove intimidation from veterinary-grade intake checklists and payment forms.
- **Dual-Mode Mental Shift:** Subtle spatial cues effortlessly transition the interface between the parent-facing booking path (calm, visual, reassuring) and the groomer workbench (dense, clear, schedule-optimized).

## Colors
The palette balances cozy hospitality with immediate operational legibility:

- **Primary (`#D96A43` Terracotta Clay):** Used for focal interactive drivers, primary booking confirmations, active timeline steps, and highlight badges. Communicates warmth, craft, and heartfelt care without the alarmist nature of pure red.
- **Secondary (`#4D7C5D` Calming Botanical Sage):** Serves as the primary indicator for confirmed bookings, completed grooming stages, healthy pet vitals, and verified intake releases.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` Warm Amber):** Reserved for contextual grooming notices, special handling notes (e.g., matted coat alerts, senior dog care requirements), and pending waitlist states.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` Slate Charcoal):** Provides deep, high-contrast readability for microcopy, schedule timestamps, breed identifiers, and client records.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering:**
  - Base canvas: `#FBF9F5` (Oat Cream) delivers a restful, paper-like background.
  - Surface elevated: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White) for distinct cards, sheets, and timeline blocks.
  - Subtle borders: `#EFE9DF` to softly separate modular sections without visual noise.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized uniformly across headlines, content, and interface tags to maintain a harmonious, geometric, and softly rounded character.

- **Chinese / Latin Pairings:** Pair with standard sans-serif system fallbacks (`PingFang TC`, `Noto Sans TC`) ensuring clean font weights and natural line rhythm across Traditional Chinese pet terminology and timestamps.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Pet names and primary status updates must always leverage semibold or bold weights (`headline-sm` or `label-lg`) to remain identifiable during high-pace salon operations.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Timestamps, pricing tiers, and weight measurements use tabular spacing numbers for clean alignment in scheduling grids.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model is tailored specifically for handheld touch interactions:

- **Mobile Viewport Structure:** Standard outer gutter and canvas padding is anchored at `1rem` (16px), giving cards full breathing room without squishing dense pet profile data.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** A cohesive 4px-derived vertical baseline ensures predictable stacking between service packages, pet intake notes, and timeline nodes.
- **Touch Ergonomics:** High-priority actions (instant rebooking, emergency contact, signature clearing) live within a 48px to 56px bottom-sheet anchor zone, easily reachable with single-handed thumb operation.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on soft sunlight-tinted ambient shadows layered on pure white cards, avoiding hard artificial drop shadows:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base):** Oat Cream (`#FBF9F5`) un-elevated background.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card Rest):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px soft border in `#EFE9DF` and a gentle warm drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(66, 45, 23, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(66, 45, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Active Grooming Node / Floating Pill):** Elevated cards, sticky appointment summaries, and active filters use `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(66, 45, 23, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(66, 45, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modal Bottom Sheets & Signature Pad Overlay):** `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.14)`.

## Shapes
The shape language is warm, soft, and tactile, matching the nurturing nature of pet care:

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Use `rounded-2xl` (16px) for cards, dialogs, and media enclosures to prevent rigid clinic vibes.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (12px) for structured, confident touch areas.
- **Badges, Filter Chips & Role Switches:** Use fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full`) for tactile feedback and effortless thumb-tapping.

## Components

### Dual-Mode Role Toggle (Owner / Groomer Workbench)
- Segmented container styled as a pill in `#EFE9DF` with 4px internal padding.
- Selected state: Pure white floating chip with soft ambient shadow and rich terracotta typography (`#D96A43`).
- Inactive state: Soft Slate Charcoal (`#64748B`), transparent background.

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Terracotta (`#D96A43`), white label, height of 48px, `rounded-xl`, subtle active scale down (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Actionable:** Sage Green (`#4D7C5D`) for positive completions (e.g., "Confirm Grooming Done", "Send Check-in Photo").
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#EFE9DF`, text `#1E293B`.

### Scheduling Timeline Component
- Vertical chronological spine in `#EFE9DF`.
- Timeline nodes feature circular status indicators:
  - *Completed (Bath/Dry):* Sage Green check with faint green background halo.
  - *In Progress (Styling/Trimming):* Pulsing Terracotta ring with white center.
  - *Upcoming (Pickup Ready):* Muted slate border with white core.
- Time tags are styled in bold mono-aligned numerals adjacent to the service card.

### Signature Pad
- Smooth canvas surface in pure white `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1.5px dashed warm outline (`#CBD5E1`).
- Contains a clear baseline guide and subtle pet paw watermark watermark in 5% opacity.
- Action bar fixed underneath with "Clear" text link and "Confirm Sign-off" primary button.

### Intake Chips & Pet Tag Badges
- Pill-shaped chips (`rounded-full`) with light tonal backgrounds:
  - *Temperament Warnings (e.g., Nervous with clippers):* Warm Amber tint (`#FEF3C7`) with `#92400E` text.
  - *Breed & Size Indicator:* Warm Stone tint (`#F1EFEA`) with `#334155` text.
  - *Vaccination Verified:* Soft Sage tint (`#DCFCE7`) with `#166534` text.

### Inputs & Selection Cards
- Form inputs feature floating labels, 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, transitioning to a warm Terracotta glow on focus (`outline: 2px solid rgba(217, 106, 67, 0.2)`).
- Selection tiles for service add-ons (spa treatment, paw balm, deshedding) include thumbnail imagery, service title, pricing, and a custom circular checkbox.
